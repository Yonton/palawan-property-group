Coron, in northern Palawan, is famous for its shipwreck diving, crystal lagoons, and dramatic karst islands. It's also quietly becoming one of the Philippines' most interesting property markets. If you're researching **Coron, Palawan real estate** for a holiday home, a resort, or a long-term investment, here's what you need to know in 2026.

## Coron vs. Busuanga: know the geography

A common point of confusion: most "Coron" tourism actually happens on **Busuanga Island**.

- **Coron Town (Coron, Busuanga municipality):** the main hub — port, restaurants, dive operators, and the jump-off for island tours.
- **Busuanga (the wider island):** home to Francisco B. Reyes Airport (USU) and large tracts of land suited to resorts and estates — including beachfront with genuine privacy.

For investors, Busuanga's larger parcels and shoreline are where the resort and estate opportunities tend to be. Our [titled beachfront estate near Coron](/listings/hotel-sa-coron) sits in exactly this kind of location.

## Why investors are looking at Coron

- **Tourism growth:** Coron consistently ranks among the Philippines' top island destinations, drawing both domestic and international visitors.
- **Undersupply of quality accommodation:** demand for well-run resorts and villas outpaces supply, supporting strong rates and occupancy.
- **Air access:** daily flights from Manila and Cebu make Coron far more reachable than its remote feel suggests.
- **Relative value:** compared with Boracay or parts of El Nido, larger beachfront parcels in Busuanga can still be found at attractive prices.

## What does property cost in Coron?

As with anywhere in Palawan, price depends on **title, access, and shoreline**:

- **Inland and near-town lots** are the most affordable and suit homes or small developments.
- **Beachfront and titled estates** carry a premium — they're the scarcest and most in demand for resort use.
- **Untitled / tax-declaration land** is cheaper but carries more risk and a longer path to a transferable title.

A clean **Transfer Certificate of Title (TCT)** is the most important value driver. We never recommend buying purely on a tax declaration without a clear, costed path to titling.

## Best uses for Coron property

- **Boutique resort or dive lodge** — Coron's diving and island-hopping demand is year-round.
- **Vacation home or villa** — a private beachfront retreat with strong rental potential when you're away.
- **Land banking** — holding a titled parcel in a growth corridor while values appreciate.

## Doing due diligence in Coron

Remote islands reward careful buyers. Before you commit:

1. **Verify the title** at the Registry of Deeds and get a certified true copy.
2. **Confirm boundaries** with a licensed geodetic engineer — island surveys can be dated.
3. **Check access** — road, water, and power availability hugely affect development cost.
4. **Understand ancestral domain and protected areas** — parts of Palawan fall under environmental and indigenous-land regulations.
